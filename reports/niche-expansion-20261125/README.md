## Added cron themes

- `game_story_competitive` — MLBB/Free Fire, one match moment with real stakes,
  original characters and script; no win claims or tutorial promises.
- `podcast_kisah_hidup` — reflective life-story framing, no invented quotes,
  names or claims; dialogue is original, not copied from a third-party podcast.

Both join the normal cooldown rotation, have their own signals, hashtags,
playlists and short/long roles, and remain behind the usual QC and rights gates.

## Director decisions recorded here

- Do not reuse recap channels as footage sources; keep them reference-only.
- Prefer measured demand, then originality, then safety — never the reverse.
- No revenue, FYP or view guarantee is attached to any new niche.
