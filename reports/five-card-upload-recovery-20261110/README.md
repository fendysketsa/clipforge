# Five-card recovery and cron vocal effects

The five local jobs were audited against their current upload records. The raw before snapshots are local diagnostics and excluded from Git. A read-only browser audit used its own tabs only and did not select media, save metadata, change visibility or press Post. It confirmed `tbAoI9h_Gis` already Public with matching saved title; `FjGZqDI8x0w` still showed the filename title. The first TikTok content page did not show the requested captions; that absence cannot authorize retrying an uncertain Post.

The fixes serialize browser uploads across platforms and compute a restart-safe gap from actual terminal finish timestamps: 45 seconds after success, 90 after failure by default. Queue entries remain queued until their turn, and cancellation/deletion stops waiting work. TikTok prepares a trial click or verified native keyboard focus before logging intent; it performs one real action, with no alternate action after an uncertain result. YouTube fills and reads the exact visible metadata field; recovery preserves the stored ID and recognizes already-Public exact-ID, exact-metadata outcomes without reapplying stale scheduling.

Cron now fills missing first-upload records for completed eligible outputs belonging to terminal scheduled campaigns. It respects live campaign checkpoints, review/News holds, normal rights/QC gates, platform readiness and any previous real upload record. Retry/reconciliation owns previous attempts. Two explicit operator-owned requests in backend/data permit one new analysis attempt for each old zero-candidate failure; child lineage is durable and the source processing ledger still enforces a total of two real attempts. No score or rights flags are promoted, and a failed child cannot get another rescue.

Singing detection uses sustained melodic pitch plus timed vocal-text evidence; dialogue merely mentioning singing and instrumental-only audio do not suffice. Floating note and melody-orbit artwork is authored procedurally, uses no copied assets, adds no music or lyrics, and shares the 1-2 accent budget with shout cues. Recitation, prayer, grief and sensitive contexts abstain. The singing demo is an illustrated synthetic calibration scene, not footage of a singer. Its encoded variants preserve frame count, duration, geometry and exact audio packets.

The new source/tests are ready, but deployment and all-five remote success are not yet verified. Activation needs interactive sudo. Use the combined activation script with --resume-cron; the default without that flag preserves the current frontend OFF switch. Existing IDs, checkpoints and browser sessions are retained. A test pass or local queued request is not evidence of an upload.
