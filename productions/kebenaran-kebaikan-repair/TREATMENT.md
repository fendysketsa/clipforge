# Direction and property continuity

The breakfast beat must read as an encounter on a market pavement. Dimas carries
a plainly visible takeaway breakfast packet. The elderly woman waits near the
curb with her two shopping bags and wooden walking cane. Dimas's gaze connects
her position to his approach. Both people and the curb/road must share one
medium-wide composition. Morning market stalls provide place and depth.

Casting for the repair sample: Dimas is a young Indonesian adult with short black
hair, charcoal-gray T-shirt and dark trousers. The grandmother is an Indonesian
woman aged 75 wearing a beige headscarf and taupe clothing. These are production
design choices for this fictional film, not additional source facts. Preserve
them in any subsequent repaired scenes.

Property master: the food belongs to Dimas; the bags and cane initially belong to
the grandmother. The cane supports her on the pavement. The assistance beat must
show a credible transfer of the bags and actual arm support. A close portrait
cannot replace the action or hide the relevant props. Crossing remains on this
same road; it does not move to a boat or a different location.

The sample retains original narration and phrase captions from the actual master.
It demonstrates repaired picture direction, not a claim that all nine scenes or
the complete film have been rerendered or approved.
