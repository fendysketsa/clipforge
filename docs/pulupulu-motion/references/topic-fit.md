# Which subjects this pipeline can carry

Owner: which subjects this tool must refuse

Decide the subject before designing anything. The engine draws type, layout, charts, diagrams and
rebuilt UI as HTML/CSS/SVG and moves them with GSAP. It has no footage, no rigs, no facial
expression, and no physics — so a subject that needs any of those will be hand-drawn geometry
pretending to be the real thing, and the note that comes back is "the result is very bad" — the
user's own words — not "needs polish".

## The gate

A brief passes if it satisfies **at least two of three**:

1. **Numbers** — specific figures that can be displayed, not opinions.
2. **Process** — a sequence, mechanism, comparison or architecture that can be drawn as boxes and arrows.
3. **Real material** — UI, logo, product shot, photo, map, screenshot of the actual thing.

Fail all three and the answer is a different pipeline, offered up front:

- **Footage-led** — archive/stock/presenter clip + TTS narration + captions, motion graphics used only
  for the bumper, chapter cards and number cards.
- **Stills-led** — 10–20 real or generated stills moved with slow Ken Burns pushes and type, again with
  motion graphics only at the joins.

Write that recommendation as a choice, with the topic the user brought, rather than silently building
the version that will fail.

**History is not the problem — people are.** An origin story passes the gate when its *objects and
beats* are drawable: a game made of falling blocks, a protocol, a spreadsheet, a machine outline, a
route on a map, a date chain, a signed document. It fails when the beats are people doing things that
a rider, a court or a crowd has to perform. Test it per beat, not per genre: write the storyboard rows
first and ask of each "can this row be a shape I can draw?" — the row that needs a face or a physical
action is the row that comes back as ugly.

**Verify every date and figure before it becomes a beat.** Take them from an encyclopedic source plus
one news or primary record, and drop anything that only appears in listicles — narration states a
number as fact, so an unverified figure is a defect in the film, not a footnote. When a number cannot
be confirmed, say the thing it implies instead of the number.

## Fits well

**Formats (the engine's own vocabulary)** — single-stat data story · chart race / bar race · vertical
timeline · count-up stat cards · before/after split · flowchart of a process · mechanism diagram ·
UI walkthrough · versus comparison on fixed criteria · kinetic typography of a quote or term ·
quiz/reveal · news ticker with one number per headline · simple map data · spec reveal · pricing
cards · leaderboard · glossary of terms · year in review.

**Technology & product** — feature launch on real UI · changelog/release notes · system architecture ·
AI benchmarks (latency, price per token) · hosting/CDN failure chains · attack flow of a breach ·
five-step setup tutorial · cloud plan comparison · privacy policy explained.

**Science & education** — one physical concept as a diagram · why an integral is an area · what sleep
deprivation does · sugar in one drink vs the daily allowance · why a city floods each rainy season ·
solar-system scale · historical numbers (casualties, distances, costs) · word origins.

**Business & marketing** — sales funnel arithmetic · margin and cost breakdown of one product ·
CPM/CPC/ROAS across platforms · why a page does not rank · monthly client report · 60-second pitch ·
supplier price comparison.

**Social, policy, news** — employment or wage statistics by region · application flow for a public
program · personal income-tax calculation · teacher-to-student ratios · waste/plastic numbers vs
solutions · a daily 45-second news recap.

**Sport (statistics, not biography)** — season recap · head-to-head numbers · how a new rule works ·
fixture card with times.

**Lifestyle & services** — menu or catalogue with prices · how-to steps · destination cost comparison ·
property listing figures · vehicle spec sheet · member onboarding steps · warranty/refund policy ·
safety SOP.

**Internal & personal brand** — townhall in five numbers · first-day induction flow · client
quotations as three cards · "three things I learned" · launch countdown · event rundown.

## Does not fit

- **Biography of a person with no footage.** Needs archive, faces and editing rhythm; drawn stick
  figures read as a school project.
- **Ancient or pre-photography history.** No contemporary imagery exists, so every object is invented
  geometry; keep it to maps, dates and numbers, or move to the stills-led route.
- **Character acting** — a figure that has to walk, react, hold an expression, or carry an emotion.
- **Photoreal or human faces.**
- **Machines performing physical action as the main idea.** A vehicle works as a *graphic beat* (a
  slide, a pose, a silhouette on a palette) but not as footage of riding.

## Indonesian channel fit (this user)

Strongest by far: **daily news recap in numbers**, **product launch on real app UI**, and
**funnel/margin/SEO explainers for clients** — all three already have the numbers and the material,
so nothing has to be invented. Biography and legend subjects are the ones that keep coming back as
rejections; route those to footage-led or stills-led before spending a build.
