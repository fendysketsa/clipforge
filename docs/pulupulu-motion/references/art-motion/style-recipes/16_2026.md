# 16_2026 · 2026 flat vector illustration + the film's punchline ending

All performance is written in **global frame numbers `F = t*60`**; key numbers are copied item by item from `../breakdown/shot-list.md` §4 so they can be checked frame by frame against the original.
Contact sheets (original left | rebuild right): `../render/16_2026/cmp_E1…E4_*.jpg`, clip `clip_2026.mp4` (10.8–15.06s).

## Style (Dribbble flat + grain)
- Solid colour blocks, no outlines; volume is separated only by "one step darker blocks of the same hue" (sweater's lit facet `#f08b7d`, cat's thigh `#f3ad4c` + one dark arc edge).
- **Grain stippling `stipple()`**: scatter 2px square dots inside the shape, density as a function of position — pink circle `0.12 + 0.75·clamp((y-cy+120)/420)` (denser the further down), lilac block denser toward bottom-right, sun denser toward bottom-right. All static parts go into the cache `fl_bg`.
- After the character layer is drawn, a static grain texture is composited `source-atop` (5% dark dots α70, 3.5% light dots α90), so grain lands only on the characters.
- Light cone: 26000 points, width ramping linearly with y from 56→150, opacity falling off with y, denser near the centre, cached as a 340×420 texture, swinging with the pendant lamp about (1010,0).

## The girl (custom pose on top of RIG)
- Torso/neck anchors: `RIG.girl({s:1.12})`; the head from a second `RIG.girl({s:1.344, dy:43.7})` — equivalent to scaling the head 1.2× about the neck point, the big-head proportion of flat illustration. Face, bangs, back hair, bun, eye/lip/ear anchors all come from it.
- Arms: `RIG.ik2(shoulder, target, 150, 140, -1)`, three target sets blended by progress: typing (both hands rise and fall alternately 7px on the keyboard at 22 rad/s) → petting the cat (688–704, near hand traces small circles on the cat's back) → gasp (791–798, `ease.outBack` lifts to in front of the chin, fingers spread).
- Expression: closed-eye arcs + upturned mouth (<686) → dot eyes + highlights, small "o" mouth (from 686, the o pops with outBack) → big eyes + wide open mouth + tongue (from 791) → after frame 838 changes to a squinting "<" (the original's final frame is also eyes closed).
- Tension lines: 3, radiating from above and in front of the head, length popping with outBack; colour 793 pale pink → 796 full red (RGB interpolation); 830–888 random jitter ±2.5px every 2 frames, head jittering ±2px in sync.
- Trouser legs/socks/shoes drawn by hand (RIG has a long skirt).

## Cat (pose library + interpolation)
Each pose is a set of local key points (facing right, origin = centroid): body 10 points, white chest 6 points, thigh ellipse, 3 back-stripe segments, head, two front legs, hind leg, tail as 4 Bézier points, gy (sole to centroid).
Poses: `SIT_F` (sitting on the floor, measured from the original at 10.95s), `SIT_T` (sitting on the table, measured facing left in the original and mirrored to facing right for storage), `LEAP` (stretched horizontally), `LAND` (front paws down). `mixPose` lerps value by value.
Transform: `translate(centroid) → [about sole or about centroid] rotate → scale(dir·sx, sy)`; squash and stretch are always about the sole, so the feet never leave the ground when it crouches.

**The key to hitting the cup**: compute the lean from world distance first, apply the lean transform, then convert the cup's right rim back into local coordinates with `c.getTransform().invertSelf().transformPoint(cup rim)` as the paw target. Computing from unleaned coordinates directly, the paw ends up 60px low after a 0.38 rad lean and stabs into the tabletop.

## Cup and shattering
- Cup x: linear interpolation through the 16 measured points in the storyboard (744:981 … 796:801); outward tilt -0.12 starts at 790–796.
- Fall 796–815: Catmull-Rom through the 9 measured centre points (measured gravity ≈1.75px/frame²), rotation `-0.12 - 2.3·q^1.3`; 6 coffee droplets behind it take the cup's position 1.3 frames earlier, smaller the earlier they are.
- Lands at 815: 7 impact radiating lines fade over 8 frames; the two large shards follow Lagrangian parabolas through the storyboard's three points (818/824/834), stopping after they land at 834; 9 small crumbs on parabolas, each stopping at its own landing height 893–911; the coffee stain widens 818:140 → 822:220 → 826:235 (ease.out); two grey ripples expand to 560/460 wide, fading out before 838; half a handle + tea-bag tag left on the floor.
- Swat motion: 3 short lines right of the cup, expanding and fading out over 12 frames after impact.

## Environment secondary action
- Pendant lamp: `damped(F, 686, 0.055rad, period 26 frames, decay 22 frames) + damped(F, 830, 0.045, 22, 20)`, the light cone swings with it.
- Plants: `damped(F, 697, 0.035, 22, 18)` (sway) + `damped(F, 821, 0.05, 9, 26)` (shake, short period); each monstera leaf also gets its own sway at a different phase.
- Cloud drifts left at 15px/s (0.25px/frame); the little plane at 177px/s with a dashed vapour trail (at 12.0s x≈440, at 13.75s x≈750, matching the original); sun rays rotate 0.35 rad/s; 17 decorative symbols boil at 4fps (±1.5px, ±0.09rad).

## Signature
`window.SIGNATURE || 'Opus 5.5'`, Caveat-600 64px scaled to 236px wide, start point (1614,1032) with a slight tilt -0.03; the writing front x is interpolated frame by frame from the storyboard 842:1606 → 870:1868, revealed with `clip`; the underline has 24 segments, stroke width thinning 3.4→1.4, drawn along the same front.
