# Treatment

A bold two-level headline establishes each idea at the top of a deep blue vertical canvas. The center becomes a directed illustrated world: objects, characters, habitats, paths, networks, machines, comparisons, and diagrams visibly act out the narrated relationship. Related beats reuse the same recognizable world while camera scale and state change with the explanation. Small grounded environment actions—moving leaves, passing creatures, light, weather, and material flow—keep the image alive without adding a factual claim. Each scene enters, performs one explanatory action, and holds its resolved picture before handing off. Spoken captions remain in one persistent low safe-area rail without disappearing between cues. Accents shift by beat while the visual grammar stays consistent. The final card lands the authored engagement line: “RESPIRASI BERLANGSUNG PADA SIANG DAN MALAM HARI, BUKAN HANYA SAAT ADA MATAHARI.”
